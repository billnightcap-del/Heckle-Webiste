## Questions for the operator

1. **Audience you already have.** Do you have an audience anywhere today that you can prove
   with a screenshot: social accounts, an existing email list, a podcast, a show you run? Which
   platform, and roughly what size? (I won't use any of it until I've seen the source.)
2. **Launch date.** When do you expect the site to be live, with analytics and a Setlist sign-up
   working? If it isn't within ~30 days, the 60-day window can't include an audience-based sale.
3. **Your network.** Are you a working comic or producer? Can you personally reach ten comics,
   and any clubs, festivals, comedy schools or bookers who'd take your call? Which city?
4. **Who may pay.** Are you open to comics paying (for example a paid festival brief), or should
   revenue come only from businesses? Are featured listings for festivals on the table at all,
   given the conflict with the Comic check?
5. **Off-limits sponsors.** Any categories you won't take: alcohol, sportsbooks/gambling,
   cannabis, crypto, AI tools, a club you have history with?
6. **What "sell" means here.** First dollar, covering monthly costs, or a repeatable deal? And
   what are the current monthly costs (domain, hosting, APIs, Claude)?
7. **Your time.** How many hours a week can you give sales calls and fulfilment? Anything that
   only works while you drive it daily gets flagged as a liability.
